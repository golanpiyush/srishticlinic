Meet your doctors

Two disciplines, one clinic. Dr. Savita Golan leads with classical Ayurvedic training focused on infertility, pain and Panchakarma; Dr. N. Golan brings 23 years of general medical practice. Patients often see both, one visit apart.

Lead Doctor · Panchakarma & Agnikarma Practitioner

Dr. Savita Golan

B.A.M.S. (Ayurved) Reg. No. 09172 C.G. Panchakarma & Agnikarma specialist

Dr. Savita Golan treats through classical Ayurveda — from infertility support to instant, effective Agnikarma pain therapy. She builds each protocol around the patient's prakriti (constitution), the Ayurvedic daily rhythm, and diet, rather than a standard chart.

  • Female & male infertility (PCOD/PCOS, tubal block, fibroid)
  • Agnikarma for arthritis, sciatica & joint pain
  • All classical Panchakarma procedures
  • Psoriasis & other skin diseases
  • Spinal cord conditions, piles, fissure & fistula
  • Diet, lifestyle & stress management consultation

General Physician & Surgeon

Dr. N. Golan

M.B.B.S. Reg. No. 5076 (IAP) Bhopal 23 years in practice

Dr. N. Golan sees general medical and minor surgical cases across all ages — from everyday illness and infections to post-operative and chronic condition follow-up. He works closely with Dr. Savita Golan on cases where infertility or pain treatment call for a combined medical and Ayurvedic approach.

  • General medical consultation
  • Minor surgical procedures
  • Chronic condition management
  • Male infertility & sperm-related issues
  • Diagnosis & referral coordination
  • Care for patients of all ages
