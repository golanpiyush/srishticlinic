What we treat

Speciality Ayurvedic & medical care

From infertility to chronic pain, our treatment plans are built around each patient — combining Dr. Savita Golan's classical Ayurvedic and Panchakarma protocols with Dr. N. Golan's clinical diagnosis.

01

Infertility Care

PCOD/PCOS, tubal block, fibroid, irregular periods, low AMH, sperm issues & genetic factors — for both male and female infertility.

02

Agnikarma

Instant, effective Ayurvedic pain management for arthritis, sciatica, frozen shoulder, cervical, knee and back pain — no injections needed.

03

Panchakarma

All classical Panchakarma procedures, advised and administered according to your prakriti and condition.

04

Skin & Spine

Psoriasis and other skin diseases, spinal cord conditions, piles, fissure & fistula treated with Ayurvedic care.

05

PCOS & Menses

PCOS, fibroid and irregular menses management, with lifestyle and diet correction alongside medicine.

06

Stress & Lifestyle

Stress management, diet consultation and lifestyle consultation rooted in the Ayurvedic daily rhythm.

07

General Medicine

Dr. N. Golan sees general physician & minor surgical cases across all ages, every clinic day.

08

Diet Consultation

Personalised diet and daily-rhythm guidance to support every treatment plan, not a one-size chart.

09

Agnikarma — Cosmetic

Agnikarma for moles, skin tags, warts, corns and tattoo marks — precise aesthetic correction, the Ayurvedic way.

10

Agnikarma — Curative

Agnikarma for abscess, piles, nail disease and more — fast, effective relief without surgery.

11

Vidhikarma

For mental health concerns, PCOD, renal stones, urinary problems, infertility, gynaecological issues, headache, frozen shoulder, back ache, sciatica & more.

12

Swarna Prashan

For children aged 0–16 years — an Ayurvedic immunity booster given as a monthly dose to support growth and health.
